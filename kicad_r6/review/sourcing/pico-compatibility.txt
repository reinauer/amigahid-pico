Pico W / Pico 2 W verification — 2026-09-29
Sources: official Raspberry Pi datasheets, figures 2 (pinout), 5 (SMT footprint), and section 2.1 (test points); https://datasheets.raspberrypi.com/picow/pico-w-datasheet.pdf and https://datasheets.raspberrypi.com/picow/pico-2-w-datasheet.pdf. Downloaded PDFs are local-only.
Both modules: 51 x 21 mm; 40 edge pins with identical GPIO/power/ground assignments, 2.54 mm pitch and 17.78 mm row spacing.
Board footprint coordinates below use module centre as origin, USB toward negative Y.
USB TP1 GND (0,-21); TP2 D- (1,-24.3); TP3 D+ (-1,-24.3); 1.5 mm square lands.
Debug D1 SWCLK (0.5841,5.7); D2 GND (3.1241,5.7); D3 SWDIO (5.6641,5.7); 1.5748 mm lands. The third debug contact is SWDIO, not another ground.
Four USB-shell solder lands: (+/-2.725,-24), 1.1 x 1.8 mm; (+/-2.425,-20.97), 1.05 x 1.45 mm. All four exist in the library AND placed U1 footprint. These unnumbered lands capture/reflow the module's existing shell solder; they have no separate carrier net assignment. Shell ground is supplied through the assembled module. TP1 and D2 have explicit carrier GND connections.
TP4/5/6 positions also match and remain electrically unconnected on the carrier.
The local footprint retains its Pico_2_W_R6 library name because it supports both modules; selected fitted Value is Pico W.
J7 HOOYA DIN-603 separately has THREE shield contacts: two side holes and a central 3 x 1 mm plated slot. All three are pad 0 and connected to GND on r6. Connector body 3D models are illustrative.
C7203003 is Pico W / RP2040, not Pico 2 W / RP2350. Firmware binaries are not interchangeable: select pico_w or pico2_w accordingly. Electrical/mechanical compatibility is not a hardware bench test.
